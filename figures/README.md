# Figures

PNG files are byte-identical to the images embedded in the submitted manuscript (figures 1–9) and Supplementary Materials (figures S1–S2); their SHA-256 values are listed in `checksums.sha256`. Figure 1 is a conceptual overview; Figures 2 and 3 are worked illustrations whose values are given in `data/examples/figure_02/` and `data/examples/figure_03/`; the source data of Figures 4–9, S1 and S2 are identified in `docs/paper_artifacts.csv` and Section S3.4 of the Supplementary Materials. Figure-generation scripts are not part of this release.
